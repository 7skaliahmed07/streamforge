# Purpose

This document defines the business goals that StreamForge aims to achieve.

These goals describe the expected outcomes after implementing a real time data platform. The goals will guide technical decisions and ensure that the platform delivers measurable business value.

---

# Primary Goals

StreamForge aims to build a centralized real time data platform that helps retail businesses collect, process, analyze, and visualize business information efficiently.

The primary goals are:

- Create a single platform for managing business data from different sources.
- Provide real time visibility into sales, customers, payments, and inventory.
- Reduce dependency on manual reporting processes.
- Improve the accuracy and availability of business information.
- Enable business teams to make faster and better decisions using reliable data.

---

# Business Objectives

The platform is designed to achieve the following business objectives:

- Reduce the time required to generate business reports.
- Improve inventory management by providing timely information.
- Provide better understanding of customer behavior and purchasing patterns.
- Improve operational efficiency through automated data processing.
- Support business growth by creating a scalable data foundation.
- Enable different teams to access accurate insights from a centralized platform.

---

# Success Metrics

The success of StreamForge will be measured through the following outcomes:

- Business dashboards provide updated information with minimal delay.
- Reporting processes require less manual effort.
- Business users can access accurate sales and customer insights.
- Data quality improves through automated validation and processing.
- The platform can handle increasing business data volumes.
- Decision making becomes faster due to improved data availability.

---

# Future Vision

StreamForge is designed to become a foundation for advanced business analytics and intelligent decision making.

Future improvements may include:

- Supporting larger transaction volumes as the business grows.
- Adding predictive analytics and machine learning capabilities.
- Expanding analytics capabilities across different business areas.
- Providing advanced forecasting for sales and inventory.
- Supporting additional data sources and business systems.

The long term vision is to create a reliable data platform that enables organizations to become more data driven.
