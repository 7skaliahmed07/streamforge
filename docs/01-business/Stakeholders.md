# Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved with StreamForge and explains their role in the platform.

Understanding stakeholders helps ensure that the platform is designed to support business needs and technical requirements.

---

# Stakeholder Groups

## Business Users

Business users consume insights generated by the platform.

Responsibilities:

- Review sales performance.
- Monitor business KPIs.
- Analyze customer behavior.
- Make data driven decisions.

Needs:

- Accurate dashboards.
- Updated business information.
- Easy access to insights.

---

## Data Engineers

Data Engineers are responsible for building and maintaining the data platform.

Responsibilities:

- Manage data pipelines.
- Maintain data processing workflows.
- Ensure data quality.
- Monitor system performance.

Needs:

- Reliable data infrastructure.
- Clear data flow.
- Monitoring and troubleshooting capabilities.

---

## Software Engineers

Software Engineers develop and maintain application services.

Responsibilities:

- Build backend APIs.
- Maintain application logic.
- Integrate business systems.
- Improve application reliability.

Needs:

- Clear API contracts.
- Good documentation.
- Stable development environment.

---

## Business Managers

Business Managers use platform insights to make strategic decisions.

Responsibilities:

- Track business performance.
- Identify opportunities.
- Plan future business activities.

Needs:

- Real time reports.
- Accurate metrics.
- Historical analysis.

---

## System Administrators

System Administrators maintain the platform environment.

Responsibilities:

- Manage deployments.
- Monitor infrastructure.
- Maintain system availability.

Needs:

- Deployment documentation.
- Monitoring tools.
- Clear operational procedures.

---

# Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder | Main Responsibility | Main Requirement |
|-------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Business Users | Analyze data | Dashboards and insights |
| Data Engineers | Maintain pipelines | Reliable data platform |
| Software Engineers | Build applications | Stable APIs and documentation |
| Business Managers | Make decisions | Accurate business metrics |
| System Administrators | Maintain systems | Reliable infrastructure |
