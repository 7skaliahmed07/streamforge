# Purpose

This document explains the business problem that StreamForge aims to solve. It describes the current challenges faced by the business and explains why a real time data platform is needed. This document serves as the foundation for all technical and architectural decisions in the project.

---

# Business Problem

StreamForge addresses the challenges faced by modern retail companies that struggle with delayed reporting and disconnected business data.

The current system has several limitations:

 - Reports are generated manually.
 - Sales data is delayed.
 - Customer information is spread across multiple systems.
 - Inventory updates are slow.
 - Managers cannot make quick decisions.

---

# Business Impact

The current situation affects the company's ability to make fast and accurate decisions. Delayed information and disconnected systems create operational problems and reduce business efficiency.

The major impacts include:

 - Lost sales
 - Poor customer experience
 - Wrong inventory decisions
 - Slow business decisions
 - Increased operational costs

---

# Current Challenges

The company currently faces several operational and data management challenges:


 - Data exists in multiple systems.
 - Reports are created manually.
 - No real time visibility.
 - Difficult to scale reporting.
 - Duplicate or inconsistent data.
 - Slow analytics.

# Expected Benefits

 - Real time dashboards.
 - Faster reporting.

 - Better customer insights.
 - Faster business decisions.
 - Improved data quality.
